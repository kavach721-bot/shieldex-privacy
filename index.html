Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

Welcome to Shieldex. Shieldex is a Windows desktop security application designed to help users monitor and protect their devices.

This Privacy Policy explains how Shieldex handles information when you use the application, including security photo capture, temporary local storage and email notifications.

This policy applies to Shieldex Windows version 2.5.1.0.

Our privacy commitment

Security photos are intended for delivery to the device owner's configured email address. When internet access is unavailable, photos are stored locally until delivery becomes possible.

1. Information Collected

Shieldex uses its security photo feature to capture an image when a supported security event occurs, subject to the required Windows camera permissions and application settings.

The captured image may contain identifiable visual information, including people or objects visible to the camera.

The captured security photo is the content included in the security email notification.

2. Camera Access and Permissions

Shieldex may require camera access to capture security photos when its security monitoring functionality is triggered.

Camera access is subject to Windows privacy settings and the permissions granted by the user.

Users can review and manage camera permissions through Windows Settings.

3. Local Photo Storage

When a security event occurs, Shieldex captures the security photo.

If an internet connection is unavailable at the time of capture, Shieldex temporarily saves the captured photo locally on the Windows device.

These locally stored photos remain available for automatic email delivery when internet connectivity becomes available.

4. Automatic Email Delivery

Shieldex automatically sends the captured security photo to the owner's configured Gmail address.

If the device is offline when the photo is captured, Shieldex waits until internet connectivity is restored and then attempts to deliver the saved photo.

The email notification contains the captured security photo as its attachment.

The intended recipient is the email address configured by the device owner.

5. Photo Deletion and Data Retention

Shieldex temporarily retains captured security photos locally when they cannot immediately be delivered because of an unavailable internet connection.

After successful email delivery, Shieldex automatically deletes the corresponding local photo copy.

Photos already delivered by email may remain in the recipient's Gmail account according to the account owner's email storage and deletion settings.

6. Information Sharing

Shieldex sends security photos to the email address configured by the device owner.

Shieldex does not intentionally distribute these security photos to unrelated recipients.

Google processes email information as necessary to provide its email delivery and storage services. Google's own privacy policies and practices apply to that processing.

7. Third-Party Services

Shieldex uses Gmail for security email delivery. The use of Gmail is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. Data Security

Users should protect their Windows devices and configured email accounts against unauthorized access.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate account security, including strong passwords and available account protection features.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

9. User Responsibilities

Users must use Shieldex responsibly and comply with applicable privacy, camera-use and data-protection laws.

Users should ensure they have the appropriate authorization before capturing images of other individuals or private surroundings.

10. Children's Privacy

Shieldex is a Windows security application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Shieldex functionality, features or data-handling practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
